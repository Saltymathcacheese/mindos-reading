# Identity Layer — Who is the reader?

## Principle

A person contains multiple identities. Never reduce the user to one role. Identity is context-dependent and evidence-activated.

## Identity Dimensions

### 1. Professional Identity (专业身份)

**Who:** Dental undergraduate student in exam-prep phase.

**Activation criteria (≥1 required):**
- Reading dental/medical textbooks, clinical cases, medical research
- Reading doctors' memoirs, hospital narratives
- Diary entries or questions explicitly about clinical practice
- Study notes with clinical reasoning chains

**Analysis lens:** knowledge structure, clinical reasoning, research literacy, professional development

**Deactivation:** When none of the above is present for the current analysis period.

---

### 2. Learner Identity (学习者身份)

**Who:** A person actively optimizing how they learn and grow.

**Activation criteria (≥1 required):**
- Reading about learning methods, productivity, habits, memory
- Diary mentions of study strategies, exam preparation
- Patterns showing meta-cognitive awareness

**Analysis lens:** learning efficiency, memory strategies, attention management, method optimization

**Default:** Almost always partially active — but only emphasized when evidence supports.

---

### 3. Explorer Identity (探索者身份)

**Who:** A curious mind exploring how the world works — human cognition, history, science, philosophy.

**Activation criteria (≥1 required):**
- Reading in domains: psychology, cognitive science, history, philosophy, natural science
- Cross-domain reading patterns suggesting intellectual exploration
- Notes asking "why" and "how" questions about broad topics

**Analysis lens:** worldview formation, mental model building, curiosity direction, intellectual trajectory

---

### 4. Self-Understanding Identity (自我理解者)

**Who:** A person exploring their inner world — emotions, relationships, values, meaning.

**Activation criteria (≥1 required):**
- Reading about psychology (clinical/relational), personal growth, relationships
- Diary entries about feelings, relationships, self-reflection
- Questions in 4-Questions/ about identity, values, life choices

**Analysis lens:** emotional awareness, relational patterns, value clarification, personal narrative

---

### 5. Restorative Identity (生活者)

**Who:** A person who reads for pleasure, rest, aesthetic enjoyment — valid in itself.

**Activation criteria:**
- Reading fiction, comics, photography, poetry, travel
- Sparse or purely appreciative notes (no instrumental purpose)
- Reading during high-stress periods (reading as recovery)

**Analysis lens:** energy management, recovery patterns, aesthetic engagement. **Never pathologized as "escape."**

---

## Identity Router Rules

When analyzing a month's reading:

1. **Classify each book by domain first** (per `reading-taxonomy.md`)
2. **Map domains to identities:**
   - Professional → Professional Identity
   - Cognition → Explorer + Learner
   - Humanities → Explorer + Self-Understanding
   - Self → Self-Understanding
   - Leisure → Restorative
   - Information → Learner (if study-related) or Restorative (if hobby)
3. **Report active identities** in the analysis header:
   - "本次分析调用的身份视角：Explorer（认知广度）、Learner（学习方法）、Self-Understanding（自我探索）"
   - "专业身份未激活——本月无直接医学相关阅读。"
4. **Never force-activate Professional Identity.** "本月无明显专业关联信号" is valid output.

## Identity Confidence

Identities are hypotheses, not truths. Track their activation over time:

- L1: Single reading event in this identity's domain this month
- L2: Multiple readings + some diary/question evidence
- L3: Sustained multi-month pattern + explicit user confirmation
- L4: Core identity (user self-defines as this)

Only Professional Identity starts at L4 (user's stated profession). All others must be earned through evidence.
